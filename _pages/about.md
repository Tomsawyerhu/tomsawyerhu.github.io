I am a Ph.D. student (2026.1–) in the Department of Computing at [The Hong Kong Polytechnic University](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/comp/), supervised by Prof. Wenjie Li. My current research focuses on building language-model systems that understand people better and provide useful, personalized support.

Before starting my Ph.D., I received both my M.Eng. and B.Eng. in Software Engineering from Nanjing University, where I was supervised by Prof. Zhenyu Chen. I also worked on large-scale database systems and LLM applications at Alibaba Cloud, Microsoft, and Alibaba.com.

## research interests

- Emotional support and personalized alignment
- Large language models and LLM agents
- AI for software engineering

I am always happy to discuss research ideas and potential collaborations. Please feel free to reach out by [email](mailto:haichuan.hu@connect.polyu.hk).
